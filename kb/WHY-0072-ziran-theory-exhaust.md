# WHY-0072: The Ziran Approach to Theory Tracking (Natural Exhaust)

**Date:** 2026-05-22
**Status:** Accepted

## Context
During an evaluation of how to scientifically test ongoing hypotheses (e.g., whether conversational claim-falsification is faster than materialized execution), a proposal was made to create an `artifacts/theories/` ledger. This ledger would have required the Agent or Operator to manually append observation data to track the hypothesis during the execution of a SPAO loop.

## Decision
We reject manual observational ledgers as fundamentally anti-Ziran. We formally codify that the tracking and testing of active "Theories" must rely exclusively on **Natural Exhaust**—the passive, ambient data trails already generated by the system's environment.

## Rationale
1. **The Friction of Bureaucracy:** True Ziran (natural flow) is violently disrupted when an operator or agent must break out of the execution loop to perform metadata bookkeeping on a philosophy. Artificial tracking scaffolding is the antithesis of flow.
2. **The Passive Sensor Paradigm:** In a natural ecosystem, you do not force a river to fill out a ledger to measure its own flow rate; you simply place a passive sensor in the current. Our Dao Engine natively generates a mathematically perfect data trail without any extra effort:
   - **The Git Ledger:** Commits and PRs natively log exact timestamps, diff volumes, and the velocity of physical execution.
   - **The Transcript Ledger:** The `.system_generated/logs/transcript.jsonl` naturally captures the exact duration, token volume, and friction of our conversational dialectic.
3. **Automated Retrospection:** To test a theory, we simply declare it once (e.g., in `artifacts/strategic_intent.yml` or a backlog Probe) and change absolutely nothing about our daily operations. Theories are evaluated retroactively by a future topological node (e.g., an Audit or Retro phase) that mathematically sweeps and scores the natural exhaust. This keeps the scientific method completely invisible and frictionless for the Operator during flow state.
