# WHY: Healing Protocol Doctrine and State of Exception

## 1. Intent
To formally codify the doctrine and non-vacuous bounds of the Healing Protocol, establishing the legitimate state-of-exception required when the SPAO loop itself is seized.

## 2. The Recovery Gap and the Resuscitator Role
SG-0005 (self-healing) presumes a *running* loop. A loop seized in its own boot layer cannot self-heal. It requires an agent external to it—acting as a Resuscitator/Healer in the Dual-Agent Paradigm (WHAT-0001 §1.2). The Healing process is an Operator + Healer dyad operating over the patient (a triad). The Healer cannot self-instantiate; the Operator summons and steers.

## 3. The State of Exception Doctrine
The Healing process is telic-Dao-aligned (passes HOW-0006) precisely *because* it restores an inoperative operational Dao. It operates in a bounded **state of exception**.
Its steps may violate operational invariants (e.g., editing `GEMINI.md` directly, acting outside the loop) only because that machinery is down.

## 4. Non-Vacuous Bounds
This exception is kept non-vacuous by three strict bounds:
1. **Triage Gate:** Falsifiable activation (only when the loop is seized).
2. **Source/Ratifier Boundary:** The Healer proposes; the Frontier Agent disposes. No self-ratification of the Dao by the Healer.
3. **Retrospective Accountability:** Formal post-mortem and evaluation.

## 5. Containment Safeguards
To prevent the exception from bleeding into the general Dao, the following properties are ratified:
- **Dormancy:** The protocol does not enter a normal Agent's baseline context; it is loadable only when the triage gate is active.
- **Inline-bound Exception:** Every Dao-breaking step must carry its triage precondition with it.
- **Reflective Rationale:** Rationale lives in immune primitives (`WHY-*`).
- **KB Purity:** No raw git/gh command strings or forbidden patterns enter `kb/` (Invariant 9).
- **Self-Declared Scope:** Healing-Protocol artifacts explicitly declare themselves as state-of-exception primitives, not general operating Dao.
