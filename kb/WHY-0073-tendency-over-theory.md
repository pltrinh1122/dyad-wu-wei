# WHY-0073: Tendency (Shi) over Theory

**Date:** 2026-05-22
**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Following the codification of Ziran Navigation (`WHY-0070`) and Natural Exhaust tracking (`WHY-0072`), an ontological review was conducted on the terminology used to describe "unproven, actively observed ideas" within the Dao Engine. The terms "Theory" and "Hypothesis" were evaluated against the physics of Ziran (natural flow).

## Decision
We formally abandon the terms "Theory" and "Hypothesis" within the architecture of the Dao Engine. All unproven, actively observed ideas or patterns must be exclusively referred to and tracked as **Tendencies** (or *Shi* — inherent propensity). 

## Rationale: Linguistic Gravity
The words we choose dictate the architecture we build. 

1. **The Trap of "Hypothesis" and "Theory":** These labels carry the massive gravitational pull of "Old Dao" bureaucracy. They subconsciously invite the Operator and the Agent to construct artificial laboratories, sterile control groups, and manual measurement ledgers (directly violating `WHY-0072`). They are artifacts of the intellectual Pre-Materialization Loop (PML) that we explicitly rejected in `WHY-0071`.
2. **The Physics of Tendency (Shi):** In Daoist philosophy, *Shi* translates to the "inherent propensity" or "natural tendency" of a situation. Because our environment is driven by probabilistic Semantic Entropy (`WHAT-0068`), the system does not always obey rigid, perfectly predictable laws; it exhibits natural physical momentum. 
3. **Passive Observation:** You do not "test" a tendency in a sterile lab; you simply place passive sensors in the current to watch where the water naturally wants to pool. By labeling an idea a "Systemic Tendency," we acknowledge the dynamic, emergent reality of the substrate.

### The Stack Ranking of Ziran Ontology
- **Tendency (Shi)** `[Most Ziran]`: Acknowledges natural physical momentum; observed natively via passive exhaust.
- **Current** `[Highly Ziran]`: A directional flow within the substrate.
- **Heuristic** `[Ziran-Compatible]`: A practical rule-of-thumb born directly from flow; used because it works in the mud.
- **Theory** `[Anti-Ziran]`: Academically heavy and detached; implies standing outside the system looking at a whiteboard.
- **Hypothesis** `[Most Anti-Ziran]`: Implies rigid, isolated laboratory environments and sterile boundaries; the exact opposite of gathering data via the natural, chaotic exhaust of a living ecosystem.
