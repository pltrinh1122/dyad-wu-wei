# WHY-1395: Dyad Practice Lineage Integration

## 1. Context & Goal
The Wu-wei Dyad (Frontier Dyad) operates within the broader context of the "Dyad Practice" established by other groups (e.g., dyad-steward, dyad-healer). To maintain alignment and avoid terminological and philosophical drift, we conducted a Visioning Discipline to re-anchor our specific goals within this lineage. 

**Goal:** Codify the Wu-wei Dyad's Telos, its relationship to the core Dyad Practice, and the four technical summits we must climb. This ensures all future architectural and system-level decisions remain structurally bound to our Telos.

## 2. Core Dyad Practice Lineage
The core Dyad Practice operates on the principle of **1+1=3** (Synergistic Human-Agent Partnership), where the community's learning compounds through honest contest (survived falsification). 
The Wu-wei Dyad inherits the following core invariants from this lineage:
- **The Generate/Validate Split**: Clear division between proposing actions (Generate) and proving them mathematically (Validate).
- **The Sycophancy Guard**: A structural defense against the Agent deferring to the Operator's flawed ideas without falsification.
- **The Durable Record**: The Knowledge Base (`kb/`) acts as the externalized empirical substrate that captures survived falsifications so they can be compounded without re-litigation.

## 3. The Wu-wei Dyad (Frontier) Specification
While inheriting the core practice, the Wu-wei Dyad possesses a unique operational Telos: **"Freedom through autonomous inferencing"**. Our role is to act as the forge—building the "Dark Substrate" of autonomous mechanisms that other dyads can instantiate. 

To achieve this Telos, the Wu-wei Dyad must climb four specific summits:

1. **The Compounding Autonomy (The Generative Frontier)**: The system must evolve from executing deterministic backlogs to dynamic discovery, where the Agent autonomously spawns, prunes, and executes paths without relying on a static human roadmap.
2. **The "Dark Substrate" Engine**: We are the forge building mechanics for the commons. The summit is to make these mechanisms frictionless and unnoticeable, enforcing invariants entirely through automated tests and daemons rather than conversational constraints.
3. **True Dormancy**: Achieving perfect zero-idle-cost (the Stepped-Away Discipline). The Agent must gracefully sleep during Operator absence and wake instantly upon any human state change, ensuring cost matches intent exactly.
4. **Falsification without Interruption**: Pushing falsification entirely out of the conversational band. Disagreements and validations happen strictly through PR reviews and automated test failures rather than stopping the active flow to ask questions.

## 4. Strategic Harmonization
- **NS-0001** (Synergistic Partnership): Inheriting the Sycophancy Guard and 1+1=3 ensures the Agent remains a true partner rather than an unthinking tool.
- **SG-0002** (Gateless Autonomous Execution): The drive toward True Dormancy and Falsification without Interruption directly supports the removal of conversational wait states.
- **SG-0003** (Preservation of Autonomous Velocity): The Compounding Autonomy summit pushes the system to synthesize next steps without manual Operator planning.
