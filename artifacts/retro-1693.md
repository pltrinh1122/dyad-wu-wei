# Epistemic Retrospective: JTBD & Dialectical Falsification Job Discipline (Node 1693)

## 1. Thesis: Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Root Integrity
In adopting the industry best practice for JTBD, we adhere to the principle of "Wu-wei" (effortless action / avoiding the imposition of a pre-conceived solution). A true Job is independent of any specific product or methodology. 

**The Constraint Axiom:**
> "The problem is not a constraint of another larger problem. If so, then the problem needs to expand in scope and be falsified."

Industry standard Job Mapping requires us to continually ask "Why?" to elevate the abstraction until we reach the **Root Functional Job**. If a defined problem is merely a symptom or a constraint of a larger unaddressed problem (a "consumption chain job"), we are not solving the true job. We must expand the scope, falsify the lower-level constraint, and target the root.

## 2. Thesis: Dialectical Falsification
Dialectical Falsification merges the Hegelian dialectic (Thesis → Antithesis → Synthesis) with rigorous Popperian falsification (actively attempting to disprove the thesis). 

When establishing the Job Discipline, we ground it in our established **Decision-Framing Discipline** (`DISCIPLINE.md`):
1. **Thesis (Propose):** Assert a single, clear disposition or Job definition.
2. **Antithesis (Counter):** Fold in its strongest possible counter-argument or falsification signal. (e.g., "Is this just a constraint of a larger problem?")
3. **Synthesis (Reconciliation):** Propose a reconciled state that survives the falsification attempt.
4. **Validation (Y/N):** Present it to the partner (the Operator) for a simple Y/N validation, making disposal cheap and keeping the contest real.

## 3. Synthesis: The Proposed Job Discipline Framework
To operationalize this, the Job Discipline template must enforce:
- **Root Problem Validation:** An explicit check that the problem is not a subset/constraint of a larger problem.
- **Solution Agnosticism:** Stripping away any implicit technical solutions from the Job definition.
- **Dialectical Framing:** Structuring the Job definition as a Thesis, providing the strongest Antithesis (why this might be the wrong job or a constrained problem), and offering a Synthesis for Y/N validation.
